BMNC / Character Workshop

Build and save characters, tune their point-buy, and export a portable file. JSON is the editable interchange format; TXT is a readable sheet.

Point-buy: Domain → ability → skill. Each level costs its level plus all earlier levels. Ordinary abilities cap at their domain; skills cap at their chosen ability. Toughness, Wound Capacity, HC, Action Capacity and Willpower are unpegged, cost 2× XP and cap at half their domain (round down). Race/body-plan tags are saved, but racial rules are not yet applied in combat. The current prototype uses a six-location physical hit table.
